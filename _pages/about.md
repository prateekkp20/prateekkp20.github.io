Hi! I'm Prateek Kumar Pandey, an AI Engineer at Genpact, and. I'm interested in computational methods for molecular simulation, statistical mechanics of chemical systems, numerical methods and high-performance computing, and machine learning for scientific computing.

I completed a dual degree (B.Tech. + M.Tech.) in Chemical Engineering at IIT Kanpur in 2025, with a minor in Industrial & Management Engineering. For my master's thesis, advised by Dr. Vishal Agarwal and Dr. Swarnendu Biswas, I developed THE2D, a method for computing electrostatic interactions in slab (2D-periodic) systems. It combines a top-hat-function formulation of Ewald summation with FFT-accelerated particle-mesh evaluation and, for a 10k-ion system, runs 13.6× faster than the standard 2D-PME method at the same accuracy. I presented this work at the IEEE HiPC 2025 workshop, and a full paper is under review at the *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation*. See my [publications](/publications/).

At Genpact, I build LLM-based and agentic AI systems for business workflows, including price-claims reconciliation, and retail pricing analytics. Before that, I interned at Colgate-Palmolive, where I built a production scheduling tool for their manufacturing lines, and at Medetronix Labs, where I worked on a prototype femtoampere current analyzer. More details are on my [experience](/experience/) page.
